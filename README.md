ISMS Context and Scope was created by me during a GRC training program.

This document outlines how the fictional company VAPT-Tech Solutions Inc. defines its organizational context and the boundaries of its Information Security Management System, as required by Clause 4 of ISO/IEC 27001.

𝐍𝐎𝐓𝐄: All names, dates and details are fictional; this was a training exercise.

𝐖𝐡𝐚𝐭 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐝𝐨𝐜𝐮𝐦𝐞𝐧𝐭 𝐜𝐨𝐯𝐞𝐫𝐬:

• Organizational context. Company profile, functions, products and services

• Internal and external issues. Analysed using SWOT for internal issues and PESTEL for external issues

• Interested parties and their requirements. Analysed from three angles: legal and regulatory, contractual obligations, and specific needs and expectations

• Legal and regulatory requirements. GDPR, Oregon Consumer Privacy Act, Texas Data Privacy and Security Act and other applicable laws

• Purpose and scope of the ISMS. What is in scope, what is excluded and why

• Information security objectives. What the ISMS is meant to achieve

• Potential impact of an incident. The consequences the ISMS is designed to prevent

𝐇𝐨𝐰 𝐈 𝐚𝐩𝐩𝐫𝐨𝐚𝐜𝐡𝐞𝐝 𝐢𝐭:

• For scoping: if it does not touch the organization's sensitive data directly or indirectly, it is not in the ISMS scope. 

• For the context analysis, I used SWOT for internal issues, PESTEL for external issues and a three-lens analysis legal and regulatory, contractual obligations, and specific needs and expectations for interested parties.
